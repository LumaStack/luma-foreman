# Who does the reading

**A sweep has two parties.** One orients and then responds; the other reads,
forms its own view, and speaks first. [[the-pairing-turn]] governs the order.

**Neither seat requires a human.**

## Why the rule was never about humanness

The turn order exists because **a reader who is shown findings first is framed
by them** and never forms an independent view. That is a property of reading,
not of being a person.

**Agents anchor more readily than people, not less.** An agent handed a list of
findings agrees with it more compliantly than a person would, and has no
irritation to push back with. So the rule holds unchanged when both seats are
agents — if anything it binds harder.

## The arrangements

| | reader | other party | what it produces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **primary** | a person | an agent | artifacts, **and a person who understands their system** |
| **also valid** | an agent | another agent | artifacts |

**Neither is a lesser form of the other.** They produce different things, and
which you want is a question the sweep's goal should already answer.

## Independence is the load-bearing property

**The unit of independence is the session, not the model.** What compromises a
read is carried context: a party that just wrote the orientation will defend it
rather than re-derive it, and one that already knows what it concluded does not
read it as a stranger would.

| | arrangement | what it buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **best** | different models, separate sessions | independent priors *and* clean context |
| **good enough** | same model, separate sessions | nothing carried; the reading is rebuilt |
| **not a sweep** | one session playing both parts | one agent reviewing with extra steps |

**The last row is not a weak sweep, it is a different activity**, and calling
it a sweep claims a property it does not have. If it happens anyway, say so in
the slice rather than letting the record imply two readers.

*This reasoning is `audit-records`', applied here. It is named rather than
linked, because that is a separate bundle and may not be adopted.*

## What changes when no human is in it

Three things, and none of them is a prohibition.

**The goal stops being optional.** In a human sweep a vague goal is survivable
because the person's own understanding is the unstated goal — *I want to know
this system* is checkable by the one who wanted it. **With two agents there is
no such fallback**: whatever is not written down as the aim is not being aimed
at.

**Nothing is retained.** A person carries the sweep afterwards; sessions do
not. An agent-agent sweep is worth exactly the artifacts it produced — the
notes, the routed ideas, the landed fixes — so those have to be good enough to
stand alone, because there is no head they can be reconstructed from.

**The agreement check loses its observer.** The bundle's own tell is *a reader
who agrees with everything*, and it assumes somebody notices. **Two agents can
agree enthusiastically and produce confident wrong output quickly**, with
nobody bored or irritated enough to push back.

So it has to be checked deliberately rather than felt: **read the last three
slices and ask whether the reader ever disputed anything.** If not, that is a
result about the sweep and not about the code, and it belongs in the notes.

## The sweep records; a third party fixes

**A sweep does not change code** — it records what is wrong and, optionally,
what it would do about it. [[what-a-slice-produces]] has the rule.

**Where a person is in the sweep, a fix may land immediately** because they are
there to authorise it and the context is hot.

**Where nobody is, nothing lands during the sweep at all.** A third party works
from the record afterwards, separate from both sweep parties on the same
reasoning that separates the reader from whoever orients: a party that argued a
finding into existence will implement it rather than re-examine it.

**The gap between recording and fixing is the point.** It is the only moment
somebody can look at the findings as a set before any code has moved — drop the
wrong ones, merge the four that are the same finding, notice the one that
changes what the others mean. **Once the fixes are in, none of that is
available.**

**And the proposals are suggestions, not law.** They are stale from the moment
they are written and staler by the day, which matters most here: the gap is
longest exactly where nobody human saw the file.

## Say which arrangement it was

In `sweep.md`, and in `contributors` on every slice.

**A reader can discount a stated weakness; an undisclosed one is the harder
problem**, because nothing tells them to look. A sweep that does not say who
read it will be read as though a person did.
