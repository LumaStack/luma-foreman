# Journal template

Copy the blocks to `.luma/backlog/sweeps/<slug>/journal.md`. **Optional** — a
sweep that does not want one does not have one. **Copy the blocks, not this
file.**

## Frontmatter

```yaml
---
type: journal
title: Journal — <the sweep>
created: YYYY-MM-DD
---
```

## Body

```markdown
# Journal

**Scratch, newest last, append-only.** Add entries; never revise them — a view
that has moved on gets a new entry saying so.

**Nothing here is meant to survive the sweep.** The close harvests every entry
into a backlog item, a learning, or a deliberate drop. What was noticed while sweeping — patterns
half-formed, things to check when the right file comes up, questions raised and
not answered.

**Not a queue.** Anything actionable goes to its real destination at the slice
that produced it. An entry still here three slices later, and actionable, was
routed wrongly.

## 2026-08-29 — slice 002

`index` may be retired vocabulary and nothing enforces it. The vocabulary rule
ran clean because the term is not configured. Worth checking against the
decision records when the `.luma/records/` cluster comes up.

Two documents now describe how `apply` reaches an agent. Not yet a finding —
watch whether a third appears.
```

**Head each entry with a date and the slice it came from.** An observation
without one cannot be weighed later, and *when did we start noticing this* is
the question a journal exists to answer.
