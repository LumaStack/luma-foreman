# Choose your model and effort once, at the start

Your conversation is cached, and cache reads cost roughly a tenth of the normal
rate. That discount is the only reason a long session does not bankrupt you.

**The model is part of the cache key.** So is your model's effort level. Change either
one mid-session and none of your history matches the cache any more, so the whole
conversation gets reprocessed at full price — ten times the cached rate.

Which means the decision in front of you is not *which model should I switch
to*. It is *which model am I using for this entire session*. Pick the model and the effort level
before you begin, and then leave both alone until the session ends. If you want
to be on a cheaper model, start there — the saving is real when you take it at
the beginning and self-defeating when you switch in the middle.

## Takeaways

- Cache reads cost about a tenth of the normal rate. That discount is why long sessions are survivable.
- **Model and effort level are both part of the cache key.**
- Pick both *before* you start a session, then leave them alone.
- Want a cheaper model? Start with it. Switching later is the expensive move.
